Unified HIS Architecture & Clinical Governance Assurance

Common database engine between Desktop ClinicDesk and Web Portal

Strict Concurrency & Duplicate Check Registration locks and phone/DOB indexing prevent duplicate patient charts and ensure single-source-of-truth medical records across all hospital endpoints.
